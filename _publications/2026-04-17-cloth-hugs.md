This preprint presents Cloth-HUGS, a Gaussian Splatting based neural rendering framework for photorealistic clothed human reconstruction that explicitly disentangles body and clothing. The model represents body and cloth as separate Gaussian layers in a shared canonical space and deforms them with SMPL-driven articulation. The paper introduces physics-inspired constraints and a depth-aware multi-pass rendering strategy enabling real-time rendering at >60 FPS. See the paper on arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.15875
