I'm a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at the [University of Texas at San Antonio](https://www.utsa.edu/), where I am fortunate to be advised by [Dr. Kevin Desai](https://klab.cs.utsa.edu/). My research lives at the intersection of generative modeling and vision-language understanding — I'm especially excited about **diffusion models**, **CLIP-style multimodal representations**, and how we can use them to give people fine-grained, intuitive control over what gets generated.

Before UTSA, I completed my M.Sc. in Electrical Engineering at [Shiraz University](https://shirazu.ac.ir/en) in Iran, where my thesis focused on **background subtraction** for video understanding under challenging real-world conditions. Outside of research, I love being on long hikes and bike rides, and I play the [setar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Setar) — a Persian lute that has kept me company through every grad-school deadline so far.

Research interests
======

- **Diffusion models** — score-based generative modeling, conditional sampling, and controllable generation.
- **Vision-language integration** — leveraging CLIP and related multimodal encoders to bridge text, images, and 3D content.
- **Human motion generation** — text-conditioned 3D human motion synthesis and motion style transfer.
